Tips for good photos:
- Plain, uncluttered background (a white/grey surface or plain wall)
- Natural daylight, not flash - avoid glare on the screen/glass
- Shoot straight-on (not at an angle) so the phone fills most of the frame
- Same background/lighting across all photos so the shop looks consistent
- If a listing has no photo yet, the site automatically shows the
  illustrated placeholder instead - nothing breaks.
